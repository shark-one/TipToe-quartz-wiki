# The Hideout
[[Lila Kartawijaya|Lila]], [[Rofiq Junaedi (Idam)|Idam]], [[Matthias Chaniago|Matthias]], and [[Nidhra Sinaga|Nidhra]]  were all close friends of [[Didit Gaib|Didit]]. They'd always found solace in playing Dungeons & Dragons (DND) together, but their late-night sessions were starting to attract unwanted attention from the adults around them. Didit's mother, in particular, had become increasingly displeased with the group's habit of gathering at her son's place until the wee hours. After a particularly grueling 6-hour session, she'd let them have it, and the friends realized it was time to take their adventures off the grid. That's when Didit mentioned an old hideout he knew just outside the neighborhood – hidden behind rows of electric pylons, out of sight from prying eyes.

The group set off with Didit as their guide, navigating through a dense thicket of foliage until they stumbled upon a thick veil of greenery. Behind it lay an old door, beckoning them to descend into the unknown — but not before a moment of hijinks.

The group descended underground, exploring the concrete-walled tunnels decorated with metal pipes and ceiling lights. They finally settled on a room and set up a game area using tables and chairs that were strangely, neatly placed there. The session was filled with laughter and camaraderie. Matthias's enthusiasm got the better of him during a backflip competition – he hit his head, resulting in a nasty gash. Nidhra sprang into action, using a machete that Lila had brought to cut away Matthias's hair, then wrapping Matthias's shirt around the wound to stem the bleeding. Matthias had achieved the perfect buzz cut.

As they wrapped up their session and began packing up, the group realized with a sinking feeling — the exit no longer existed. They could only venture deeper into the mysterious facility in search of a way out. The tunnel branched and twisted, disorienting the group. At one point, Idam and Nidhra were separated from the rest of the group and had to continue with just the two of them.

# Series of Tests
The group continued the exploration separately. They stumbled upon a room with a door. The door had multiple buttons, and only by pressing them in unison could the group gain access to the other side. There, the group was reunited. As they continued on their journey, they stumbled upon another room with a similar setup: a door and multiple buttons. This time, however, they approached with caution, having learned from their previous experience. They threw Matthias's shoe at a button, which activated a trap, and finally figured out the mechanism for the door.

 The door swung open, revealing another dark corridor beyond. As they stepped into the new room, Idam let out a gasp of shock. Behind the door stood a figure, its features obscured by shadows. But as the group watched in horror, the figure vanished into thin air.

Matthias sensed that something was amiss. He threw his other shoe into the darkness, hoping to distract whatever lay ahead. To their surprise, a childlike voice responded from the shadows. Matthias's shoe was nowhere to be seen.

# The Ventriloquist
As they navigated through the dark corridor and into another room, the group caught a glimpse of a mysterious girl standing in the shadows, sobbing uncontrollably. On her feet was Matthias's shoe, which he had thrown earlier as a distraction. Matthias quickly approached the girl, grabbing a pipe from the wall, and threw it at her. It wasn't until he heard the sobbing sound from behind him that he realized his mistake.

The girl figure began to crumble into metal parts as the group finally understood that she was just a trap. The towering dark figure they had seen before was watching Matthias closely from behind, producing the sobbing sound of a little girl from a hole between its two red eyes. 

The group scrambled to open the next door, pushing buttons to trap the figure. Finally, after what seemed like an eternity, they managed to make the door open and flee towards the tunnel, closing the door behind them. The sobbing of the little girl turned into screams as the figure scratched and clawed at the door, trying to force it open. It was clear the door wouldn't hold it much longer.

The group reached another room and succeeded in opening the next door. However, their relief was short-lived, as they soon realized that the figure was catching up to them. Panic set in, and in the chaos, the group became separated.

Each of them stumbled into different branching paths, only to find themselves at dead ends with a human-sized hole on the ground. With the figure catching up to them, each of them leapt into the holes, which turned out to be slides that carried them further underground and into a brightly lit chamber filled with row upon row of swimming pools. The air was thick with the scent of chlorine.
