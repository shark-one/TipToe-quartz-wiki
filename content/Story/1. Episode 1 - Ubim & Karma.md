# The Hideout
[[Ubim Pinata]], [[Karma Pamungkas]], and [[Didit Gaib]] were close friends who shared a passion for Dungeons & Dragons (DND). Together  they would spend hours playing DND at Didit's home. After a particularly long session that lasted 6 hours, the three friends were reprimanded by Didit's mother. Having already experienced several verbal rebukes from adults around them, Ubim, Karma, and Didit realized it was time to move their sessions elsewhere, away from the eyes of the adults. Fortunately, Didit knew just the place.

The group followed Didit as he navigated the dense foliage through rows of electric pylons until eventually stopping at an old door hidden behind a thick veil of foliage. The group descended [[2. Floor 1|underground]] until they found a room where they spent several hours playing their next session of DND within the facility, using the makeshift tables and chairs to set up a game area.

But as they wrapped up their session and began to pack up their gear, Ubim suddenly wandered off, leaving Karma and Didit with no choice but to follow. The walls around them twisted, and the group finally realized they were now trapped underground.

# Series of Tests
As they continued to explore the abandoned facility, Ubim, Karma, and Didit stumbled upon several rooms. These rooms contained a door that could only be opened by pressing multiple buttons in unison within a specific time frame. The group needed to work together. In the chaos of trying to open the door, Karma found himself trapped between two walls, barely avoiding being crushed by the sudden movement of the panels. Didit and Ubim rushed to his aid, managing to pry him free just in time. As they helped Karma to safety, Karma caught a glimpse of something out of the corner of his eye - a mysterious figure lurking behind the door before it vanished into thin air.

Without another word, the group decided to investigate further. They carefully approached the door, pressing the buttons in unison as they had before. The sound of clicking mechanisms echoed through the room as the door creaked open, revealing a narrow corridor that stretched on into darkness.

# The Ventriloquist
As they navigated through the dark corridor, Ubim, Karma, and Didit finally caught a glimpse of a mysterious girl, who was now standing in the shadows, sobbing uncontrollably. The group tried to ignore her. However it became clear that this was the same [[2. The Ventriloquist|figure]] Karma had seen earlier — a towering humanoid figure that could mimic the voice of a little girl to lure them into a trap.

After much effort, the trio finally managed to open the door leading to the next room, but their celebration was short-lived. As they burst out of the room, they found themselves being chased by the figure. In the chaos, Didit got caught and was brutally killed by his pursuer. After butchering Didit, the figure gained an unsettling ability — it could now mimic Didit's voice.
