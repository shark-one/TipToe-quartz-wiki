# The Incident

Sheila was transported to another world while on a school bus as it passed through a dark tunnel. The incident occurred during a field trip, and [[Ubim Pinata]] who was a close friend of Sheila was the sole witness of the incident.

Sheila's disappearance had a profound impact on Ubim, leading him to become more reserved and introspective. Despite his efforts to visit her home, he was met with denial by her parents, who claimed they had no daughter named Sheila.
