One of the kids who stumbled into the [[Underground facility|underground facility]]. Nidhra was a close friend of [[Didit Gaib|Didit]] and a classmate of [[Matthias Chaniago|Matthias]]. They played a regular DnD session. 

# Personality
Nidhra is a smart kid, which allows him to skip grades. Even though he was already attending high-school, his mind and body was still that of a middle-schooler. Compared to other high-schoolers he was smaller in stature and was still having "eight-grader syndrome", often referring to himself as Nidhra Drakia, just like his character in DnD.
