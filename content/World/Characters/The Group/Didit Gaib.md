One of the kids who stumbled into the [[Underground facility|Underground Facility]]. He was the one who discovered the facility and ultimately led the group inside. Didit is the Dungeon Master for the group's DnD sessions — a charismatic, confident individual with a natural talent for storytelling and world-building. He takes great pride in crafting immersive scenarios for his friends to navigate. 

Like [[Ubim Pinata|Ubim]] and [[Karma Pamungkas|Karma]] he was a school dropout and spent most of his time crafting stories and playing DND with his friends. His mother and the adults around him often scolded him about it, warning him that he needed to focus on his life rather than indulging in fantasy. As a result, Didit and his group began searching for a new hideout where they could hold their sessions without interruptions, eventually leading them to discover the underground facility.

# Discovery of the Underground Facility

Didit was the one who stumbled upon the entrance to the underground facility that the group is currently exploring. The entrance was hidden behind a thick tangle of foliage. How he found it remains unknown.
