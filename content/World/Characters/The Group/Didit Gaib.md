One of the kids who stumbled into the [[Underground facility|Underground facility]]. He was the one that discovered the facility and ultimately led the groups inside. Didit is the dungeon master on the groups DnD plays. A charismatic and confident individual who has a natural talent for storytelling and world-building. As the Dungeon Master, he takes great pride in crafting immersive scenarios for his friends to navigate. 

Like [[Ubim Pinata|Ubim]] and [[Karma Pamungkas|Karma]] he was a school dropout and spent most of his time crafting stories and playing DND with his friends. His mother and the adults around him often scolded him about it, warning him that he needed to focus on his life rather than indulging in fantasy. As a result, Didit and his group began searching for a new hideout where they could hold their sessions without interruptions, eventually leading them to discover the underground facility.

# Discovery of the Underground Facility

Didit was the one who stumbled upon the entrance to the underground facility that the group is currently exploring. The entrance was hidden behind a thick thicket of foliage. How he stumbled on the entrance was still unknown.
