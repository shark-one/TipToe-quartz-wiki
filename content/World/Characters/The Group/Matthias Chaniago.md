One of the kids who stumbled into the [[Underground facility|underground facility]]. A kid of Sumatran origin, Matthias is [[Nidhra Sinaga|Nidhra]]'s classmate. Both of them, together with [[Lila Kartawijaya|Lila]], [[Rofiq Junaedi (Idam)|Idam]], and [[Didit Gaib|Didit]], are close friends who share a passion for Dungeons & Dragons (DnD).

# Personality
Matthias is a curious and cautious child. He often engages in unconventional behavior to gather more information about his surroundings, such as licking buttons or throwing objects — sometimes leading to unintended consequences. Matthias has a tendency to exhibit poor self-preservation instincts. He is willing to take risks and approach mysterious figures without hesitation, which can put him at risk of injury or harm.

Matthias is known to occasionally bring alcoholic beverages to social gatherings with his friends. This behavior has been observed to lead to a decline in the group's collective cognitive function and decision-making abilities.

# Appearance
Before entering the underground facility, Matthias was rocking a short cut. However, during a failed backflip his head was split open, and [[Nidhra Sinaga|Nidhra]] had to cut Matthias's hair to stop the bleeding. Matthias's shirt was also used as bandages. During their time in the facility, Matthias also lost both of his shoes — used to activate traps, interact with an [[2. The Ventriloquist|entity]], and inspect holes in the ground.
