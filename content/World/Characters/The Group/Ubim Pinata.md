One of the kids who stumbled into the [[Underground facility|underground facility]]. Ubim is a quiet, reserved kid who occasionally reveals his more playful and mischievous side. This carefree nature often lands him and his friends in trouble. Like his close companions [[Didit Gaib|Didit]] and [[Karma Pamungkas|Karma]], Ubim was a school dropout who spent most of his time indulging his love for Dungeons & Dragons (DnD).

# Early Life

Ubim was a child filled with mischief and chaos. He had a close friend named [[Sheila]]. However, their friendship took a strange turn when Ubim witnessed Sheila being transported to another world while on a school bus, as it passed through a dark tunnel. Despite his efforts to warn her parents, Ubim's visits to Sheila's home were met with denial. Her parents claimed they had no daughter named Sheila. This unsettling experience had a profound impact on Ubim. He became more reserved and quiet.
