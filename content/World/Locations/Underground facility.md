The Underground Facility, also known as "The Facility", is a vast network of abandoned tunnels and rooms that crisscross beneath the neighborhood. The exact purpose and history of the facility are still unknown. 

# Discovery
The group stumbled upon the entrance to the facility while exploring the abandoned areas of the city. [[Didit Gaib]], being the curious and adventurous individual he is, discovered a hidden door behind an old electric pylon in the neighborhood. The door opened into a narrow corridor that ran down into a vast network of tunnels and rooms.

# Layout
The Facility's layout is complex. What makes it even more disorienting is that the layout of the rooms and walls appears to be constantly changing. Hallways turn into dead ends; doors lead to different areas one moment, only to disappear or reappear in another location later on. Trash scattered on the floor will vanish unnoticed and unused chairs and tables will reconfigure themselves when nobody's looking.  [[The Group|The group]] has had to rely heavily on their wits and map-making skills to navigate the ever-shifting corridors.
