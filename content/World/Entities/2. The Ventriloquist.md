The Ventriloquist is an entity the group encountered in the [[Underground facility|underground facility]]. The first time they came across it, the entity was watching and waiting for them, only disappearing once the group noticed it.

# Appearance
The Ventriloquist has a massive humanoid body with inhuman proportions. It tends to hide itself in shadows, making it difficult to discern any features beyond its dark surroundings. However, two distinctive asymmetric red eyes can be seen. Between these eyes are holes that the Ventriloquist uses to imitate the voices of those it has killed.

# Abilities
- **Voice Mimicry**:
  The Ventriloquist is able to imitate the voices of people it has previously killed. It was observed using the voice of an unknown little girl — ranging from sobbing to pleas for help to screams — to lure the group into traps. It also imitated [[Didit Gaib|Didit]]'s voice after killing him, in an attempt to lure [[Ubim Pinata|Ubim]] and [[Karma Pamungkas|Karma]]. It seems able to imitate voices regardless of whether it ever observed the subject saying the phrases it imitates. For example, it was able to imitate Didit's pleas for help, despite Didit never having pleaded for help once.
- **Sculpt**:
  The Ventriloquist was able to make a sculpture of an unknown little girl. By presenting the sculpture and imitating a voice, it tried to lure the group into traps. The sculpture was made from scrap metal seemingly lying around the room. Even so, [[Matthias Chaniago|Matthias]] managed to take the sculpture down with a pipe he threw. 
- **Lethal Strike**:
  The Ventriloquist was able to strike down Didit in one hit, killing him instantly.
