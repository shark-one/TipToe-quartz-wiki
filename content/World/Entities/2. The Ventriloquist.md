The Ventriloquist is an entity that the groups had encountered in the [[Underground facility|underground facility]]. The first time the group had encountered the entity, it was observing and waiting for the group, only disappearing when the group had noticed the entity.

# Appearance
The Ventriloquist has a massive humanoid body with inhuman proportions. It tends to hide itself in shadows, making it difficult to discern any features beyond its dark surroundings. However, two distinctive asymmetric red eyes can be seen. Between these eyes are holes that the Ventriloquist uses to imitate the voices of those it has killed.

# Abilities
- **Voice Mimicry**:
  The Ventriloquist is able to imitate the voice of the people it has previously killed. It was observed using the voice of an unknown little girl, which ranges from sobbing, pleas for help, and screams to lure the groups into traps. The Ventriloquist was able to imitate [[Didit Gaib|Didit]]'s voice after it had successfully killed Didit to try and lure [[Ubim Pinata|Ubim]] and [[Karma Pamungkas|Karma]]. It seems to able to imitate voices regardless of whether it had observed the subject using the phrases that it imitates or not. For example, it was able to imitate Didit's pleas for help despite Didit never pleas for help once.
- **Sculpt**:
  The Ventriloquist was able to make a sculpture of an unknown little girl. By presenting the sculpture and imitating voice it tried to led the groups into traps. The sculpture was made using metal junks that was seemingly laying around in the room that The Ventriloquist had made. Despite that, [[Matthias Chaniago|Matthias]] was able to took the sculpture down using a pipe that he had thrown. 
- **Lethal Strike**:
  The Ventriloquist was able to struct down Didit in one hit, killing him instantly.
